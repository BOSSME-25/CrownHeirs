Team Hub

Every booking, cancellation, completion and no-show made here is pushed to Team Hub — it takes the seat HighLevel had, so payroll reconciliation, today's outlook and service hours keep working. A stylist can also take their hours and time off from the hub (Team → edit → “Hours come from Team Hub”).

Set TEAMHUB_URL (e.g. https://team.crownheirs.com) and TEAMHUB_SECRET — the secret shown on the hub's Admin → HighLevel page — in Vercel, then redeploy. Then in the hub's Admin → HighLevel table, give each stylist their id from this site's Team tab as the calendar ID and their work email as the user ID; that's how the hub credits appointments to them.
