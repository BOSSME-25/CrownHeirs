Booking database

Online booking and the front desk run on Postgres. Press once after connecting a database in Vercel, and again after any update that changes the schema. Safe to press any time — it never overwrites edited prices or deletes appointments.

Notifications

Configured in Vercel → crown-heirs → Settings → Environment Variables (then redeploy). Each channel stays silent until its keys exist.
RESEND_API_KEY
NOTIFY_FROM_EMAIL		Email confirmations, cancellations and reminders via Resend. The from-address needs a verified domain in Resend.
TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID
TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN
TWILIO_FROM_NUMBER		Text messages via Twilio. US business texting needs Twilio's A2P 10DLC registration first.
NOTIFY_EMAIL
NOTIFY_SMS_TO		Where the salon's copy of each booking and cancellation goes.
CRON_SECRETAny long random string. Turns on the 9 AM daily reminder run for the next day's appointments.
