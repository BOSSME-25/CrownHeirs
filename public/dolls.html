Meet the maker

Coletta Peterson

Crochet designer and maker behind A Touch of BLU: “Black and Brown dolls for Black and Brown people.”

Coletta picked up crochet to beat the boredom that set in after losing a job she loved. She mastered the basics with endless hats, dish towels and scarves, then made her first bespoke doll for an expectant friend.

She puts a small piece of herself into every doll, and sees each one as “a showcase for the beauty and style of an all too often underrepresented people.” Every Crown Heirs doll you build here is made by her hand.

“Doll making gives me the freedom to be all of the things that kid me wanted to grow up to be: a ballerina doctor who is also a firefighter and a veterinarian.”Coletta Peterson
