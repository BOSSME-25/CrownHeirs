About Us

Love, service and excellence.

Established in July 2021, we welcomed the public into what is often called "The Den" on September 1st of the same year. Our vision: a professional yet comfortable full service salon where love, service and excellence are the pillars of the hair haven, and a hub for community resources and economic development.

The Den is a community anchor that delivers support, education and services. We provide a space for self care for diverse cultures, with clients ranging from toddlers to seniors, unique employment opportunities and training, and we intentionally connect with correctional rehabilitation clients.

Our vision

We are a community anchor for partnerships that propel our community forward. We do so by advancing individuals to their highest levels of health, wealth and wellness through resources, training, financial literacy education and scholarship.
