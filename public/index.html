Our Story

Love, service and excellence

Established in July 2021, we officially welcomed the public into what is often called "The Den" on September 1st of the same year. Our vision for the community: a professional yet comfortable full service salon where love, service and excellence are the pillars of the hair haven, and a hub for community resources and economic development.

Our salon serves as a community anchor that delivers support, education and services. We provide a space for self care for diverse cultures, with clients ranging from toddlers to seniors. We also provide unique employment opportunities and training, and we intentionally connect with correctional rehabilitation clients.

Our Vision

We are a community anchor for partnerships that propel our community forward. We do so by advancing individuals to their highest levels of health, wealth and wellness through resources, training, financial literacy education and scholarship.

E

Emily Belt

Co-Owner and Chief Executive Officer

Ms. Belt is known to most as a pillar of gratitude, humility, consistency and, most importantly, unwavering faith. For the past 12 years she has been a proud resident of Laveen, Arizona, where she lives with her all-academic, all-pro, entrepreneur daughter, Elisabeth. As a proud member of Zeta Phi Beta Sorority, Inc., her focus is being an asset to her local communities. Her experience includes community and program development, advocacy and specialist work in foster care, counseling with a focus on behavior modification, and serving as a liaison for diversity development in higher education.

Emily is an experienced leader, coach and team developer with a special interest in operational development, strategic planning and growth. Although her education and early career are rooted in social services, she has shown that her skills transfer to any industry and stage. Alongside leading The Den, she serves as Associate Vice President of Enrollment Operations at Grand Canyon University, where she has proudly worked for 15 years.

She has been privileged to serve some of the most dynamic and diverse groups of people, and she leaves each role confident they will continue to soar. She is eager to work alongside phenomenal people already doing great work, and to partner with others to break new boundaries, overcome new challenges and exceed expectations.

B

Bethany Grissum

Chief Operating Officer

Bethany has been an independent salon stylist since 2019, specializing in loc maintenance and care with an emphasis on natural hair restoration. An Arizona native, she earned her Bachelor's in Biology with an emphasis in Pre-Medicine from Grand Canyon University, then continued into Master's study in Public Administration with an emphasis in Health Care Administration. Her education and passion for health and wellness, fueled by her faith, led her to create House of Figs LLC, a functional nutrition company. It brings together her creative work as a portrait photographer (Third Wheel Photography, LLC), private chef (Ms. Baes LLC), loctician (CandetLocs, LLC) and published model.

While she has a drive for entrepreneurship, she has a passion for change and is an advocate for self development that stems from purpose. She feels privileged to be part of the positive side of others' journeys. She has a deep community service background and has spent her life in ministry, at home and abroad, with a special focus on youth development and care for widows across Arizona.

In addition to her time in the salon as Chief Operating Officer, she manages an admissions team for Grand Canyon University, where she has managed various departments for the last decade. Bethany is a woman of faith who aims to be an exemplary resource for innovation, stability and renaissance.
